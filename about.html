The FOOTRA Journey

FOOTRA was founded by a passionate entrepreneur who wanted to create a unique and high-quality leather goods brand. What started as a small workshop with just three craftsmen has grown into a premier footwear destination, serving thousands of satisfied customers worldwide.

Our mission is simple — to design footwear that combines style, strength, and comfort. Our boots are crafted for individuals who walk their own path and define their own style. We believe footwear is more than fashion — it's confidence, identity, and attitude.

"Every pair of FOOTRA shoes tells a story of craftsmanship, dedication, and the pursuit of perfection. We're not just making footwear; we're building confidence, one step at a time."

— Rahul Mehta, Founder & CEO
