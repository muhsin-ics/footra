Our Story: Bold Leather Footwear, Made to Last

FOOTRA crafts boots and shoes for people who walk their own path. Step into confidence with every stride.

The FOOTRA Journey

FOOTRA was founded by an entrepreneur who wanted to build a high-quality leather goods brand. It began as a small workshop with three craftsmen and has grown into a footwear destination for people who value thoughtful design and lasting quality.

Our mission is simple: design footwear that combines style, strength and comfort. We believe footwear is more than fashion. It is confidence, identity and attitude.

“Every pair of FOOTRA shoes tells a story of craftsmanship, dedication, and the pursuit of perfection. We're not just making footwear; we're building confidence, one step at a time.”

— The FOOTRA Team
